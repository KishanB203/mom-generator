What it does

A small icon next to the clock. No meetings bot, nothing to remember.

Teams meetings, automatically

Join a meeting and recording starts. Leave it and the MoM is created. Speaker names come from the Teams window.

In-person meetings

Click the icon to record the room with your laptop microphone. Click again to stop.

Two files, every time

A "who said what" transcript in the language it was spoken, plus the English MoM, saved in Downloads\MoM_Notes.

English, Hindi, Gujarati

Each sentence is written in its own script, then translated, so nothing said in Hindi or Gujarati is lost.

Private by design

Audio is turned into text on your PC and never uploaded. Only the text goes to Claude, with your own account.

You review, then send

The MoM opens in Notepad. Unclear points are marked (?) so you can fix them before sharing.

Install in 3 minutes

No admin rights needed.

  1. Download and double-click MoMGenerator-Setup.exe If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
  2. Click InstallThe app starts by itself when the install finishes.
  3. Follow the setup window It installs Claude Code if needed, opens your browser to sign in with your Claude account, and downloads the speech model (1.6 GB, once).
  4. DoneA short guide shows how to start and stop. Your notes appear in Downloads\MoM_Notes.
You need a Claude account with Claude Code access (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise plan) to write the MoM. An Anthropic API key works too.

Questions

Is it free?

The app is free. Writing the MoM uses your own Claude plan or API key.

Does it work on Mac?

No, Windows 10 and 11 only.

Do I have to start it before every meeting?

No. Tick "Start MoM Generator when Windows starts" during setup and Teams meetings are caught automatically.

How long does the MoM take?

Speech is converted to text during the meeting, so the MoM is usually ready 1–3 minutes after you leave.

How do I update or uninstall?

Download and run the latest setup over the old one; your notes and settings stay. Uninstall from Windows Settings → Apps.

Should I tell people I'm recording?

Yes. Always tell participants the meeting is being recorded, and follow your company's recording policy.
